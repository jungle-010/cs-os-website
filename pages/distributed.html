distributed

definition

where an OS makes a group of separate computers, connected by a network, work together as one system by sharing tasks and resources between them

benefits and drawbacks

benefits

  • workload is shared, so large tasks finish faster due to increased processing power & load balancing
  • scalable - good for enterprise environments
  • redundancy included - if a machine fails, other systems can keep going allowing for perpetual uptime

drawbacks

  • high maintenance required
  • a single network issue may cause a bottleneck, affecting everything
  • keeping all systems synchronised may be difficult

examples

  • proxmox nodes / vmware esxi nodes
  • kubernetes clusters
  • general server clusters

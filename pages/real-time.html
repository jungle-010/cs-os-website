real time

definition

where an OS processes input/output within a guaranteed time limit where late responses could cause faliure

benefits and drawbacks

benefits

  • response times are predictable and guaranteed
  • very reliable - suited to mission critical applications
  • can handle continuous input from sensors

drawbacks

  • expensive to design and test
  • specialised, causing a potential lack of features
  • potential bugs can cause catastrophic faliures

examples

  • aviation/flight control
  • airbags
  • vxworks
